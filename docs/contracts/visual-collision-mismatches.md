# Visual ↔ collision mismatches (handoff to Codex)

Audit of art against the collision inputs on `feat/forest-rpg-v2-foundation` (0596ad9). The counts come from seeds 1, 42 and 1337 (level 1, normal) plus the tutorial. Each row says whether the renderer fixed it or the simulation owns it.

| # | Asset / terrain | Mismatch | Evidence | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FOREST / BORDER trees (`tile_tree`) | Trees were drawn only on even (x, y). The 64×64 sprite covers columns x-1..x+1 and rows y-1..y, so the odd last row/column of a forest region, and 1-tile pockets at odd coordinates, were **solid grass: invisible walls** | Uncovered solid forest tiles: seed 1 150/596, seed 42 157/638, seed 1337 196/1182, tutorial 20/100 | **Renderer fixed.** `planForestTrees` adds filler trees until every solid forest tile is covered (`tests/unit/rendering/forestTrees.test.ts`). Collision unchanged. |
| 2 | PLATEAU (hill interior) | Solid in `TILE_PROPERTIES` but drawn as grass tinted `0xeff3d8`, so it looked walkable | 6,046–7,839 PLATEAU tiles per generated map (about 30% of the area) | **Renderer fixed:** raised plateau-top art from the new cliff tileset. Codex's contract confirms hills are solid, including during jumps. |
| 3 | Hill rims (HILL_*) | 96×33 / 34×92 / 77×78 rim art squashed into 32×32 tiles, with no cliff face. Concave corners became PLATEAU with no rim, leaving open corners | 261–376 concave-corner cells per map | **Renderer fixed:** 47-mask blob autotiler with south cliff faces, inner/outer corners and contact shadows. |
| 4 | Decor obstacles (`log_*`, `bush_obstacle`, `log_stump`) | Drawn `contain`-scaled inside their tile, but the old collision blocked the whole tile (`WorldProp.solid`) | `log_horizontal` 48×33 is drawn 32×22 px at the tile bottom, so the whole-tile collider extends 10 px above the visible log. `log_vertical` 32×48 is drawn 32×48, so its top half-tile overhangs the tile above without blocking it | **Simulation:** Codex's `OBJECT_FOOTPRINTS` (trunk/log bounds) supersedes this. The debug overlay takes them through `setDebugColliderSource`. |
| 5 | `rock_boulder` | Opaque stone with baked grass tufts at its base. Walkable before (decor), but reads as solid | Recording 12-47-52 0:00–0:07 | **Simulation:** Codex now makes boulders solid ([0.1,0.4]–[0.9,1]). Art kept; the grass tufts are part of the sprite, not an alpha error (alpha is binary). |
| 6 | Portal (`portal_forest`) | The art used to be bottom-anchored at the tile **centre**, so the arch floated half a tile up from its entry point | `PixiRenderer` before: `sprite.y = e.y * 32` | **Renderer fixed:** the art base sits on the tile bottom; the steps cover the 0.8-tile entry radius. |
| 7 | Portal entry radius | `0.8` was a literal in `Game.fixedUpdate` | Renderer needs it for transition detection | **Simulation:** Codex exposes `PORTAL_RULES.entryRadius` (0.8). After the merge, import it in place of `PORTAL_ENTER_RADIUS_ASSUMED`. |
| 8 | Player feet | Collision AABB halfSize 0.3 is centred on (x, y); the sprite's feet sit 10 px below (x, y) (`CHARACTER_FOOT_OFFSET_PX`) | Visible in the debug overlay (cyan box against the sprite) | Acceptable: the box covers the lower body. Codex may prefer a foot-centred box; the renderer has no change to make. |
| 9 | Diagonal hill exploit | The user saw players slip through diagonal hill joins | Reported by the user | **Simulation:** Codex's swept hill collision (`bb46b45`). The renderer now draws concave corners as closed rims, so a diagonal gap no longer *looks* passable. |
| 10 | Projectiles | Every projectile arrives as `spriteKey: 'projectile'`, so the renderer drew `Texture.WHITE` (the pale-blue rectangle in recording 12-47-52 @ 0:06) | `Simulation.buildRenderSnapshot` | **Renderer fixed** using the `projectileType` field. **Request:** set `spriteKey` to the projectile type. |
| 11 | Woodland trees (`codex/gameplay-repair` v4 generator) | **User-reported (2026-10-28):** "I can still walk behind solid objects like the trees." Trees became props with a trunk-only collider ([0.25,0.55]–[0.75,1] of the trunk tile). The 64×64 canopy covers columns x-1..x+1 and rows y-1..y, so the player walks under and behind the canopy. The user expects trees to be solid. | User screenshot (sparse woodland, player standing among canopies) | **Simulation (Codex): requested.** Make the tree collider cover the visible tree body: at least the trunk tile in full, plus the canopy row above (y-1, columns x-1..x+1), or whatever footprint the user approves. The renderer will keep drawing the canopy over the player while a collider allows any overlap. |
