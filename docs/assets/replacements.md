# Asset replacements and additions

| Asset | Status | Source / ownership | Replaces |
|---|---|---|---|
| `public/assets/images/terrain/hills/cliff_tileset.png` + `.json` | **New** (360×180 RGBA; 50 frames of 32×32 on a 36 px pitch with 2 px extruded gutters) | Generated by `scripts/build-hill-tileset.mjs` from code alone, so it is project-owned. The palette comes from the project's own `hills/hill_north.png` (rock tones). The plateau-top leaf pattern is the project's `terrain/grass.png`, remapped to brighter tones. No third-party art. Re-run with `node scripts/build-hill-tileset.mjs`. The output is deterministic. | The rendering use of the 8 legacy rim images `hills/hill_{north,…}.png`. Those files are kept (they are still in the asset manifest bundle and are the palette source) but terrain no longer draws them. |
| Magic bolt, glow, spark, ring and leaf textures | **New**, runtime only (`src/rendering/effects/proceduralTextures.ts`) | Drawn on 2D canvases at startup. The bolt follows the legacy `PROCEDURAL_SPRITES.magic_bolt` spec. | `Texture.WHITE` for projectiles and screen-space ellipses for leaves. |
| Missing-art placeholder | **Changed** (`TextureStore`) | Runtime 16 px magenta/black checker | A plain white 16 px square, which read as a floating white artifact |

**Not replaced (audited):**
- **`portal_forest.png`** is still the portal frame. The `portal_active`/`portal_inactive` sheets are crude placeholders (a dark rectangle and a cyan/purple pattern), so they stay unused. The portal states are presentation layers over `portal_forest`: a masked rotating vortex, a colour grade, glow and sparks.
- **`rock_boulder.png`** has binary alpha. The green tufts at its base are painted art, not a background left over from bad transparency.
- **`objects/clouds/cloud_{1..4}.png`** are kept. They render as soft-focus sky clouds with ground shadows.
- **The coin spin (`coin_anim.png`)** is a legitimate yellow element and is unchanged.
